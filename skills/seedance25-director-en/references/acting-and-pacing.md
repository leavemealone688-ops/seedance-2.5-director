# Acting and Pacing

## Natural acting

- **No deliberate, on-the-nose symbolic gestures**: pocketing money for the audience, pointing at the bench, solemnly placing a keepsake, staring into the lens, a slow-motion ceremonial kneel. AI video amplifies these into "acting".
- Actions should be what this person would really do right here: "Cole simply eases himself down off the tailgate and settles on one knee, the practical way a man would get closer to a dog on a truck bed; he does not drop both knees, bow, or make a show of it."
- Write emotion as a **small visible reaction chain**: trigger → one small action → landing. "Cole slowly lifts his eyes to her, a small breath, then looks down at the dog."
- Write `subtle, understated reaction, no exaggerated acting` — but don't write only emotion words ("sad", "moved"); write actions.
- Eyelines land on the other person, not the lens (unless the script says so).
- Every prop and action needs a story reason; no reason, cut it.

## Tight pacing

- Write `brisk natural pacing with no idle pauses and no ceremonial business`.
- Something must happen in 0-2 s (an entrance, a line, an action); never open on an empty frame.
- React immediately after the line, wrap the reaction in 1-3 s; **no long staring hold at the end** — the last beat gets "one breath", 1 second.
- In a 10 s clip: entrance ≤ 2 s, main event 4-5 s, reaction 2-3 s.
- Rhythm comes from contrast: fast, short beats in conflict; a little breathing room at the emotional landing. Ratios matter more than absolute seconds.

## Emotion → camera language

| Feeling | Angle / framing | Light | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pressure, threat | Low angle, close | Top or rim light, low key | Locked off or very slow push |
| Small, scrutinized | High angle, wide | Cool, flat | Locked off |
| Intimacy | Eye level, close, shallow focus | Warm side light | Very slow push in |
| Tension, escape | Follow, medium | Flickering practicals | Gimbal backing away |
| Relief, warmth | Low medium | Cool turning warm | Slight drop or pull back |

One main move per beat.

## Self-check

- If you cut this action, what does the story lose? Nothing → cut it.
- What is the audience waiting for in this pause? Nothing → cut it.
- Is the actor "performing for the camera"? Make it "doing it for the other person".
