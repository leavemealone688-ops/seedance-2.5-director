# Fights and Action

Complex action and body contact are the least stable thing in AI video and the most tightly moderated. Principle: **shoot action through blocking and reactions, not the contact itself**.

## Writing

- One action per beat, 2-3 s per beat; write each as "start → motion → end pose".
- Lock direction: "he charges from frame left to frame right".
- Replace contact with restraint: a hand pins a wrist, shoves a door, grabs at a collar and stops; "a gap always remains between them, no body contact".
- Shoot the reaction, not the hit: show the swing, cut to the receiver's reaction (stumbles back) — no fist landing on a face.
- Feet and boots out of frame to avoid "stomping" imagery.
- Weapons only as silhouettes or glints; fantasy powers as light, wind, ash.
- Sell speed with camera and environment (flickering lights, dust, debris), not rapid combos.
- Hands in medium shots, simple fists and wrist grips, no complex finger work.

## Shot design

- Action plays better as multi-shot short beats (2-3 s each, hard cuts) than as a one-take.
- Pre-visualize complex blocking with gray-box / previs and attach it as "camera and blocking only".
- At most one person attacking per clip.

## Moderation

- No blood, wounds, kill, death; write a fall as "falls and stops moving", or cut away.
- Horror monsters and ghost hands: "gray-black, translucent, smoky hands" that never touch anyone.

## QC

- Pull frames at ~6 fps to check limb counts, clipping, prop teleporting.
- Direction of action matches the previous shot.
