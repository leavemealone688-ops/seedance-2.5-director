# Template: script → shots → generation units

## Step 1: one scene

```
Scene 03　Clinic entrance　Cool morning mist → warming　Present: Edna, Cole, Bo (dog)
Purpose: with one line, Edna gets Cole to let go of his fear (payoff: Cole looks up)
Action: Edna walks from the porch to the pickup and stops beside Cole.
Dialogue: Edna (warm, slightly husky older female voice): "Don't let fear be what he sees. Let him see his partner."
Reaction: Cole looks up at her, takes a breath, looks down at Bo.
```

## Step 2: break into shots

| Shot | Framing / camera | Content | Opens from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3A | Medium / truck right | Edna enters, stops | End of the previous scene, Cole on the tailgate |
| 3B | Medium close / same side | Edna's line | End of 3A |
| 3C | Medium / same side | Cole's reaction | End of 3B |

## Step 3: merge into generation units

Same time and space, same people, simple action → 3A+3B+3C become **U03 (10 s one-take)**:

| Seconds | Content |
|---|---|
| 0-2s | Opens on the previous shot's framing; Edna enters from the porch side and stops beside Cole |
| 2-7s | Edna looks at Cole and says the line; only Edna speaks |
| 7-10s | Cole looks up, one breath, looks down at Bo; no long staring hold |

## Step 4: write the prompt

Use `shot-prompt-domestic.md` or `canvas-prompt-overseas.md`.

## Step 5: self-check

- Only one main thing per beat?
- Does the opening match the previous shot's end?
- Any on-the-nose symbolic gesture?
- Which second is the payoff? Is a question left at the episode's end?
